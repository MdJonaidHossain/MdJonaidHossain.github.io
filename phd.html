Sugar transporters and CO2 acclimation in plants

2025 – present · Institut Jean-Pierre Bourgin (IJPB), INRAE Versailles · Université Paris-Saclay · with Dr Rozenn Le Hir

Rising atmospheric CO2 hands a plant more carbon than its metabolism is built to spend. Photosynthesis speeds up; growth does not follow at the same rate. The surplus has to go somewhere, and in a leaf cell the place it goes is the vacuole — the large central store whose membrane, the tonoplast, is studded with SWEET2, SWEET16 and SWEET17, the transporters that fill it and drain it again.
